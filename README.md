# 🐣 The-TamaGochi
**Your pet to keep alive!!**

## 📜 Instructions

1. Press the **bottom-middle button 5 times** to hatch the egg.  
2. Your **TamaStudi** will appear and begin its life with a **happy mood**.  
3. The first mood is always **happy**, because TamaStudi starts with full lives.  
4. Soon after, your pet will express its **first need**.  
5. TamaStudi has **3 moods/needs**:
   - 🎮 **Playing**
   - 🍖 **Eating**
   - 💩 **Pooping**

6. Based on its current need, press the correct button:
   - 💩 **Toilet** → for poop needs  
   - 🧽 **Sponge** → for cleaning  
   - 🍖 **Meat** → when hungry

7. ⚠️ **Be quick and accurate!**  
   - Pressing the **wrong button** or taking **too long** will cost your TamaStudi **one life**.

## 🎯 Goal

**Keep your TamaStudi alive as long as possible!**  
How long can you keep it happy and healthy?
